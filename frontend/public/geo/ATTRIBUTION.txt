NSW local government area boundaries (nsw_lga.geojson)

Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics, "Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 3",
Jul2021-Jun2026 (2025 update), LGA 2024 boundaries.
https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs-edition-3/jul2021-jun2026
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. (c) Commonwealth of Australia 2021-2025.

Obtained via the `nswgeo` R package (https://github.com/cidm-ph/nswgeo), which simplifies the geometry
(750 m tolerance). Further simplified here (~400 m) and re-wound for D3 to keep the file small.
Names have the " (NSW)" suffix removed. Two non-spatial categories (no usual address; migratory) are omitted.
